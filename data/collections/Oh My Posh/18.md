On this page

A [Homebrew](https://brew.sh/)
 Formula and Cask (macOS only) are available for easy installation.

    brew install jandedobbeleer/oh-my-posh/oh-my-posh

This installs two things:

*   `oh-my-posh` - Executable, added to `$(brew --prefix)/bin`
*   `themes` - The latest Oh My Posh [themes](https://ohmyposh.dev/docs/themes)
    

If you want to use a predefined theme, you can find them in `$(brew --prefix oh-my-posh)/themes`, referencing them as such will always keep them compatible when updating Oh My Posh.

Updating[​](https://ohmyposh.dev/docs/installation/homebrew#updating "Direct link to Updating")

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    brew update && brew upgrade oh-my-posh

tip

In case you see [strange behaviour](https://github.com/JanDeDobbeleer/oh-my-posh/issues/1287)
 in your shell, reload it after upgrading Oh My Posh. For example in zsh:

    brew update && brew upgrade && exec zsh
